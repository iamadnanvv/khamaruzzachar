Our story

A taste of home, from Kerala

Meet the people, place, and traditional recipes behind every jar of Khamaruzz Nadan Achaar.

A family tradition

Made to bring the flavours of home a little closer.

Khamaruzz Nadan Achaar grew from founder Kamarunneesa Firoz's love of Kerala's homemade achaar and the recipes that bring family around the table. The name “Nadan” is a nod to the homestyle flavours and familiar traditions at the heart of our food.

Today, Khamaruzz Foods makes Kerala-inspired pickles and kondattam with the same respect for tradition. We keep the story simple: a good recipe, thoughtful preparation, and a jar meant to be shared.

Every household has its own way of enjoying achaar. We hope our collection feels like a welcome addition to yours.
