Before you order

Shipping & returns

Delivery arrangements are confirmed with you directly before an order is accepted. Here is how to check the details.

Delivery coverage

We aim to serve customers across India, but delivery serviceability depends on your PIN code and the selected product. Please message us with your PIN code before ordering so we can confirm.

Shipping charges & timelines

Shipping charges and estimated delivery dates are not fixed on this website. We will confirm both for your address before you approve an order. Dispatch and delivery estimates may vary by location and courier.

Order confirmation & tracking

Orders are placed through WhatsApp. Your order is confirmed only after Khamaruzz acknowledges it and agrees the product, total, delivery charge, and address with you. If tracking is available for your shipment, request the details from us on WhatsApp.

Cancellations, returns & refunds

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order; a cancellation may not be possible once an order has been prepared or dispatched. Because these are food products, contact us before returning anything. If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or with a quality concern, message us promptly with your order details and photos so we can review the issue and discuss an appropriate resolution.

No return, refund, or replacement is automatic. Any resolution will be discussed with you based on the circumstances and applicable consumer law.
